Docker container with DockTail

{{template "snippet" "labels:\n - docktail.service.enable=true\n - docktail.service.name=myapp\n - docktail.service.port=8080"}}

With an OAuth client, DockTail also defines the Service. Without one, define it on the Services page first.

Any app with tailscale serve

Define svc:myapp with port 443 on the Services page, then run this on a tagged machine:

{{template "snippet" "tailscale serve --service=svc:myapp --https=443 127.0.0.1:8080"}}

Then approve the machine on the Services page. The setting survives restarts; keep the app running with Docker or systemd.

App on its own tailnet device

If the app answers https://<device>.<tailnet>.ts.net/ on port 443 with a valid Tailscale certificate (TSDProxy v2, tsbridge, ScaleTail, tailscale serve on the device) and tailhome's node may reach it, it appears here on the next refresh. Other ports and paths are not discovered.
